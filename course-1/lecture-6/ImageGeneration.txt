How AI Generates Images
* It is trained on millions of images
* It builds from scratch, pixel by pixel


GANs [Painter]
* It stands for Generative Adversarial Networks
* Generator: produces fake images designed to look real [G]
* Discriminator: tests each image, spotting fakes vs real
* Adversarial Process:A continuous feedback loop that sharpens both - like an endless art battle

VAEs [Architects]
* Compress, Imagine, Rebuild
* It compresses an image into a thing called Latent Space
* It takes sample points from this Latent Space to create new realistic images
* It is great for generating smooth controlled variations

Diffusion Models [Sculptor]
* AI learns both to destroy (add noise) and rebuild (remove noise)
* Slower than GANs
